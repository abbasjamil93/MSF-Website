Our Story

A foundry's discipline.
A global reach.

M.S. Fittings Mfg. Co. Pvt. Ltd. was established in May 1957 by a group of vastly experienced and enterprising founder partners. It was a humble start — a machine shop at 17, Weston Street, Calcutta 700 013. That same address still carries our registered office. In our business, that continuity is unusual. To us, it is everything.

Slowly and steadily the firm progressed — and in the last six decades, by leaps and bounds. Today the Budge Budge works house forging, forming, bending, CNC machining, heat treatment and inspection — every stage under one roof — producing quality forged pipe fittings and flanges conforming to national and international specifications.

A Symbol of Quality & Reliability — the same commitment held since 1957. — M.S. Fittings Manufacturing Co. Pvt. Ltd.

Quality Control

Zero tolerance for non-conformance.

Every batch leaving our works carries full material traceability — from billet heat number through every manufacturing stage to final dispatch. Experienced inspectors are present at every operation. No rejection at final stage is our standard, not our aspiration.
